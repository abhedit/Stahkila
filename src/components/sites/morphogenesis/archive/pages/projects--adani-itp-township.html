Adani ITP Township

Mumbai

  • Site Area: 191.95 acres

Where Every Path Leads to Community

The Adani ITP Township reimagines the contemporary township as an interconnected urban landscape, where movement, nature, and community form the foundation of everyday life. Rather than conceiving development as a collection of isolated residential enclaves, the masterplan establishes a cohesive framework of streets, public spaces, green corridors, and civic destinations that work together to create a vibrant and walkable urban environment.
Structured around a hierarchy of pathways, districts, nodes, edges, and landmarks, the development is anchored by a central city centre that acts as its social and commercial heart. A network of shaded boulevards, pedestrian promenades, cycling routes, and green fingers extends outward from this core, stitching together residential neighbourhoods, commercial precincts, educational institutions, healthcare facilities, recreational landscapes, and waterfront destinations into a seamless urban experience.
The design places human interaction at the forefront. Amenities are distributed within comfortable walking distances, encouraging active lifestyles while reducing dependence on vehicular movement. Landscaped open spaces and water bodies are integrated as essential components of the urban fabric, shaping microclimates, enhancing environmental performance, and creating a distinctive identity rooted in place.
Guided by principles of sustainability, accessibility, and long-term resilience, the township leverages climate-responsive planning, smart infrastructure, and a finely grained public realm to elevate the quality of urban living. Streets are conceived not merely as corridors of movement, but as social spaces that foster exchange, activity, and community engagement.
The result is a contemporary urban district that transcends conventional township planning, demonstrating how thoughtful design can transform land into a connected, inclusive, and enduring place where every journey, whether across a boulevard, through a green corridor, or toward the waterfront, ultimately leads back to community.
