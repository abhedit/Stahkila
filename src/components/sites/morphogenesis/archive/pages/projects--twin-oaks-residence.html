Twin Oaks Residence

New Delhi

  • Site Area: 11,000 sq. ft.
  • Built Up: 36,000 sq. ft.

A twin residence where sculpted volumes and screened transparency shape a quieter luxury

Set within one of Delhi’s most established residential enclaves, Twin Oaks Residence is conceived as a contemporary private residence composed as two duplex homes held within a single architectural language. Rather than relying on overt spectacle, the house derives its presence from proportion, restraint, and the careful calibration of openness and retreat. Its twin character is not simply formal; it becomes the basis for a more nuanced domestic arrangement in which individuality and shared identity remain in deliberate balance. The modern residence is designed as two private duplex homes, articulated through a palette of grey stone, wood, and glass.

The architecture is defined by floating horizontal slabs, warm vertical screens, and transparent layers that give the residence both clarity and depth. Clean lines and measured proportions establish a quiet assurance, while the material palette lends the house warmth without excess. The massing is further shaped by its relationship to the ground, where landscaped terraces and stepped planes create a more gradual transition into the site, softening the scale of the built form and enriching the experience of arrival.

The private residence is marked by the subtlety with which privacy is orchestrated. Openness is never indiscriminate; it is filtered through screens, recesses, and layered thresholds that allow the house to feel luminous and outward-looking without sacrificing discretion. Luxury is expressed less through display than through control, material refinement, and spatial composure. The result is a residence of notable poise, where contemporary domestic life is articulated through balance, tactility, and an exacting sense of architectural restraint.
