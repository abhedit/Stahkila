Titan Regional Office

Kolkata

  • Built Up: 20,000 sq. ft.

A workplace shaped by Kolkata’s cultural soul and Titan’s legacy of craft, transforming the office into a living community.

The Titan Regional Office in Kolkata reimagines the contemporary workplace as an immersive environment rooted in community, heritage, and craftsmanship. Conceived as more than a conventional corporate office, the design draws inspiration from the cultural character of Kolkata and Titan’s longstanding legacy of design excellence to create a warm, inclusive, and deeply human-centred workspace.

The design philosophy is anchored in the idea of “designing a workspace for living,” where collaboration, interaction, and comfort become central to the spatial experience. Open collaboration zones, informal meeting spaces, hospitality-inspired lounges, and flexible work environments are woven together to encourage spontaneous engagement and foster a strong sense of community across teams. The office becomes a social landscape rather than a static corporate setting, reflecting Titan’s people-first culture.

The interiors reinterpret Kolkata’s art deco heritage and material warmth through a contemporary lens. Arched niches, patterned surfaces, earthy textures, handcrafted screens, woven elements, and layered material palettes create a tactile and culturally resonant environment. Titan’s brand identity is subtly integrated through geometric abstractions and strategic use of brand colours within furniture, partitions, and feature walls, allowing the workspace to feel distinctly identifiable without overwhelming the overall spatial character.

Craft and creativity emerge as defining spatial themes across the office. Custom-designed partitions, textured surfaces, handcrafted details, and collaborative hubs introduce rhythm, porosity, and visual depth throughout the workplace. Cafeterias, touchdown areas, and collaborative lounges are designed as active social anchors, reinforcing the office as a vibrant and connected ecosystem. Through its fusion of cultural memory, contemporary functionality, and brand expression, the Titan Regional Office creates a workplace experience that is rooted in identity while fostering openness, creativity, and belonging.
