Trevoc Royal Residences

Gurugram

  • Site Area: 2.1 acres
  • Built Up: 8,00,000 sq. ft.

A vertical garden in the sky

TREVOC Residences, the flagship residential development by the Spaze Group, redefines contemporary luxury living in one of Gurugram’s most established neighbourhoods. Located in Sector 56, with seamless access to road and metro networks, the project combines urban connectivity with the tranquillity of a private enclave. Set across a compact 2-acre site, it houses 168 exclusive 3- and 4-bedroom residences ranging between 2,500 and 3,300 sq. ft., each designed to evoke the experience of living within a vertical garden.

The architectural strategy rises from a clear vision: to create an oasis amid density. Two slender towers emerge gracefully above a landscaped podium, their staggered configuration optimizing light, air, and views. The podium itself functions as a green plinth, transforming the ground plane into a continuous landscape. Beneath it, stilted gardens and shaded courts weave a seamless connection between built and natural realms. Lobbies, lounges, and the reception extend into this verdant under croft, dissolving boundaries between interior and exterior space.

Each residence is conceived for exclusivity and comfort. Only four apartments occupy a typical floor, each accessed directly by a private elevator that opens into the home. With wrap-around balconies and floor-to-ceiling glazing, every unit enjoys abundant daylight, cross-ventilation, and sweeping skyline views. The result is a layout that feels open yet intimate, where privacy coexists with transparency.

At the heart of the project lies a layered approach to leisure and landscape. From the club reception, express elevators ascend to the podium-level clubhouse, further connecting to rooftop sky gardens that crown each tower. These elevated greens provide visual continuity, linking ground to skyline through a vertical cascade of landscaped terraces. Within this framework, the architecture unites innovation, environmental responsiveness, and human well-being, redefining the paradigm of urban luxury in India’s National Capital Region.
