Gurugram

  • Site Area: 8 acres
  • Built Up: 14,00,000 sq. ft.

A Vertical Nieghbourhood of 360 Sky Bungalows

Mahindra Luminare reimagines the experience of bungalow living within a contemporary high-rise community. Located in Gurgaon, at the edge of the Aravalli hills, the development responds to a growing demand for urban density without relinquishing the openness, privacy, and connection to nature traditionally associated with individual homes. Conceived as a collection of 360-degree sky bungalows, the project offers an alternative model for vertical living, one that preserves the spatial generosity of a villa while embracing the efficiencies of collective housing.

The architecture is shaped by both climate and culture. In a context where residents often maintain strong connections to outdoor living, the project seeks to sustain the relationship between inside and outside rather than replace it. The ground plane is liberated for landscape and shared community spaces, while the towers are positioned to capture prevailing monsoon winds and minimise exposure to the harsh western sun. These strategies establish a comfortable microclimate while strengthening the connection between architecture and landscape.

The development comprises three residential towers, each organised around four L-shaped corner residences per floor. This configuration gives every home dual orientations, maximising daylight, cross ventilation, and uninterrupted views in multiple directions. A split-core system with private lobbies reinforces a sense of individuality and arrival, creating an experience more closely associated with standalone homes than conventional apartment living.

Central to the design are the deep verandas that wrap each residence. Conceived as elevated extensions of the domestic realm, they provide shade, rain protection, and usable outdoor living space throughout much of the year. A central courtyard uses the stack effect to moderate wind conditions at height, allowing homes to remain naturally ventilated and connected to the outdoors. The result is a vertical neighbourhood that translates the qualities of the bungalow, openness, privacy, landscape, and climate responsiveness, into a contemporary urban form.
