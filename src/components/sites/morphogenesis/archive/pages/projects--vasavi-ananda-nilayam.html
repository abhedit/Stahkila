Vasavi Ananda Nilayam

Hyderabad

  • Site Area: 29 acres
  • Built Up: 1,21,46,000 sq. ft.

India’s largest residential community on a single layout

Vasavi Ananda Nilayam in Hyderabad reimagines large scale housing through sustainability, cultural sensitivity, and human centered design. The project treats climate as both an environmental and social force, using orientation, landscape, and spatial planning to create a residential environment that feels contemporary yet rooted in place. The result is a community where architecture and ecology work together to elevate daily life.

A defining feature of the masterplan is its commitment to openness. Seventy seven percent of the site is reserved for landscape and shared spaces, shifting emphasis from roads and infrastructure to community life. With road coverage reduced by 80 percent, more land is freed for gardens, walking loops, and social spaces that encourage outdoor activity. The Grand Central Boulevard acts as the ceremonial spine of the project, connecting the towers while creating a shaded, pedestrian friendly public realm that is safe and accessible for all ages.

Climate responsiveness is central to the design. Tower placement reduces harsh solar gain and captures prevailing winds, lowering outdoor temperatures by five to seven degrees Celsius. Tree canopies, shaded courts, and generous setbacks further improve thermal comfort and reduce the urban heat island effect, making open spaces feel like natural extensions of the home.

Sustainability is embedded as a system. Passive design keeps energy use within 35 to 40 kWh per square meter, while naturally ventilated corridors and corner units maximize daylight and airflow. Water management is equally robust, led by a 1,200 cubic meter rainwater sump and reuse systems that support irrigation. The Vasavi CARE landscape adds over 130,000 square feet of shaded communal space, enhancing biodiversity and well being.

Architecturally, the development blends modern expression with subtle local references. With corner units, Vaastu aligned planning, wellness amenities, and inclusive play areas, Vasavi Ananda Nilayam offers a compelling model for responsible and community oriented living in India.
