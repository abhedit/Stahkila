Government Office Complex

Bhutan

  • Site Area: 19 acres
  • Built Up: 7,36,500 sq. ft.

A contemporary civic landscape where governance, culture, and public life converge within the spirit of Bhutanese identity.

The Government Office Complex in Thimphu is envisioned as a contemporary interpretation of Bhutan’s civic and cultural identity, bringing together governance, public life, and landscape into a unified urban framework. Located within the Thimphu valley and framed by forested mountains, the development consolidates multiple ministries into an interconnected and future-ready campus while responding sensitively to the ecological and cultural significance of its setting.

The masterplan is guided by the idea of governance as an open and accessible civic experience. A landscaped public spine weaves through the site, connecting ministries, convention facilities, plazas, and shared public amenities into a continuous pedestrian environment. Along Norzin Lam, the project establishes an active urban frontage that extends the life of the city into the campus through shaded walkways, hospitality spill-outs, and public gathering spaces. At the heart of the development, a convention centre acts as a civic connector, creating opportunities for dialogue, cultural exchange, and public engagement between citizens and institutions.

The architectural language reinterprets traditional Bhutanese elements such as Rabsel windows, timber brackets, sloped roofs, and whitewashed facades through a contemporary lens. Modular planning systems allow ministries to adapt and evolve over time, while interconnected bridges foster collaboration across departments. Responding to the terrain, the design minimizes cut-and-fill by aligning built forms with natural contours and integrating landscape into the daily experience of the campus.

Conceived as a net-zero-ready development, the project integrates passive solar orientation, thermal mass optimization, rainwater harvesting, natural ventilation, and rooftop solar infrastructure to support Bhutan’s carbon-positive vision and commitment to sustainable growth.
