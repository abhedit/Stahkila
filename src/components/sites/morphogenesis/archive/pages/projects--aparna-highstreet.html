Aparna Highstreet

Hyderabad

  • Site Area: 5.2 acres
  • Built Up: 2,00,000 sq. ft.

A retail destination conceived as an urban oasis of landscape, spectacle, and slow leisure

Set within Gachibowli, Hyderabad, at the centre of a larger emerging masterplan, Aparna Deccan Town High Street Retail is envisioned as far more than a commercial precinct. The proposal seeks to shape a new kind of public destination: one that draws together shopping, entertainment, food, and recreation within an environment defined as much by atmosphere as by programme. Surrounded by high-end residences, villas, and neighbouring commercial development, the site occupies a natural catchment for footfall, while the relative absence of comparable retail destinations in the vicinity gives the project a particular civic and market relevance.

The architecture is organised as a low-rise, naturally ventilated high street arranged around a preserved central spine of mother earth. Rather than allowing built mass to dominate the plot, the scheme offsets its blocks to retain a substantial green core, enabling native trees, water bodies, shaded courts, and open promenades to become the project’s defining experience. The multiplex anchors the heavier southern edge, while retail and food-and-beverage zones unfold more lightly towards the north and east, aligning the composition with both climatic logic and Vaastu principles. Across the site, punctured courts, terraced decks, and landscaped spill-outs create a sequence of movement that feels closer to a garden walk than a conventional mall interior.

Environmental performance is embedded within this spatial idea. Mutual shading, prevailing wind movement, extensive softscape, and integrated water features are all used to lower perceptible temperature and make the public realm more habitable through much of the year. Above, a rooftop sky garden with breweries, restaurants, and alfresco terraces extends the experience into a more festive social landscape. What emerges is a high street conceived with unusual ambition: commercially vibrant, climatically intelligent, and memorable for the way it turns retail into an immersive urban retreat.
