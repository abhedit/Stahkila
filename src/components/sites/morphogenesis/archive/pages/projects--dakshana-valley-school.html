Dakshana Valley School

Pune

  • Site Area: 125 acres
  • Built Up: 5,00,000 sq. ft.

Generate, Conserve, Recycle: A Closed Loop

Dakshana Valley School is conceived for gifted students from low-income backgrounds, offering an intensive residential programme that prepares them for India’s most competitive engineering and medical entrance examinations. Set within a valley near Pune, the campus is envisioned as more than an educational institution. It is a home away from home, designed to support academic excellence while fostering independence, confidence, and a strong sense of belonging.

Spread across 109 acres, the campus is shaped by the natural contours of the site. Existing vegetation and water bodies are preserved, allowing the landscape to become an integral part of everyday life. A central academic street begins at the pavilion block and extends towards the lake, structuring the campus as a gradual journey through learning, community, and recreation. Along this spine, classrooms, hostels, dining halls, and shared facilities are arranged around shaded courts, informal gathering spaces, and outdoor learning environments that encourage interaction, reflection, and peer exchange.

Environmental responsiveness is woven into the planning from the outset. Buildings are oriented to capture prevailing winds, minimise cut and fill, and reduce heat gain through earth-berming, shading, thermal mass, and carefully designed façades. More than ninety percent of the campus is naturally daylit and ventilated, creating healthy and comfortable environments for study and living. The school also targets net-zero energy and water through renewable energy generation, rainwater harvesting, reservoirs, and restored catchment systems.

Locally sourced materials including brick, basalt, stone, terracotta, and wood lend the campus a sense of warmth and familiarity. Together, landscape, architecture, and environmental performance create a setting where education extends beyond the classroom, cultivating resilience, responsibility, and a lasting connection to nature alongside academic achievement.
