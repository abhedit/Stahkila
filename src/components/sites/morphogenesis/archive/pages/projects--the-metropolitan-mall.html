The Metropolitan Mall

Gurugram

  • Site Area: 3.7 acres
  • Built Up: 5,63,000 sq. ft.

North India’s First Large-Format Enclosed Mall

The Metropolitan was conceived as North India’s first large-format enclosed mall at a time when Gurgaon was rapidly urbanising and emerging as a new commercial centre. The project aimed to create a landmark retail destination that could meet contemporary technological and commercial demands while embedding an Indian sensibility within a modern mall typology. With a programme that included a multiplex, food court and expansive retail areas, the development reinterprets the traditional bazaar as a contemporary urban activity hub.

The entrance sequence is designed to accommodate both vehicles and pedestrians, establishing clarity, accessibility and a strong sense of arrival. A landscaped granite-paved forecourt forms a generous urban plaza capable of hosting events, gatherings and informal social activity reminiscent of historic street culture. Through a frameless glazed façade, this plaza flows seamlessly into the mall’s central atrium, ensuring visual and experiential continuity between outdoor and indoor public realms.

 

The atrium functions as the heart of the project and is conceived as a protected public space. Its vaulted roof, clad in translucent panels, filters daylight to create a soft, evenly lit environment while shielding the interior from harsh solar exposure. This adaptable space supports seasonal change, festivals and spontaneous urban activity, evoking the layered, episodic character of traditional Indian bazaars within a controlled climatic environment.

Interior materials were selected for durability, low maintenance and visual clarity, aligning the project with international retail standards. Stainless steel is used extensively for cladding, railings, escalator surrounds and custom furniture, creating a cohesive architectural language. Timber and steel seating elements are distributed throughout the atrium to encourage pause and interaction. Escalators, clad in steel and accentuated by cool blue lighting, act as a dynamic focal element within the central court.
